# SEO

Metadata is centralised in `src/content/seo.ts`. Routes require specific titles, descriptions and canonicals. The app generates its sitemap, robots policy and `ProfessionalService` JSON-LD. Set a verified `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`; keep `SITE_INDEXABLE=false` through staging. Enable indexing only after the content, domain, form delivery and legal review are complete.
