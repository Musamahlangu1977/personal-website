# Brand

Mahlangu Online Solutions is a career and personal branding studio founded independently by Musa Njabulo Mahlangu in Pretoria in December 2023. Its central promise is: “We open doors. You walk through — with the documents to prove it.”

The brand is strategic, credible, human, modern and accessible. It treats career documents, personal identity and digital presence as one connected system. Never reduce the studio to inexpensive CV formatting. Use calm, assured language and supported claims.

The current website direction is a monochrome digital studio experience: pure black stages, white editorial typography, muted grey support text, liquid-metal controls, fine grid lines and restrained light effects. The visual identity should feel cinematic, focused and technically precise. Do not reintroduce the former forest-green and gold website palette unless the owner explicitly requests it. Source portfolio work may contain its original project colours, but website presentation should be grayscale or heavily desaturated so the site remains coherent.
